You're the center fielder. The stadium lights are on, the stands are packed, and the fly balls just keep coming.
100 of them. Can you catch every single one?

■ READ IT, RUN, LAY OUT
• Read the direction and height off the crack of the bat and get a good first step
• Deep drives over your head — start back early or you won't get there
• Sinking liners in front of you — lay out for a diving catch
• Balls at the wall — leap at the fence and rob the home run
• Later on, the wind starts pushing the ball around

■ 3D VIEW FROM BEHIND THE CENTER FIELDER
• Like a home run derby seen from the outfield
• Track the ball with the batter, pitcher, packed stands and light towers right in front of you
• Use the ball's shadow on the grass and the mini-map to find the landing spot

■ FIREWORKS AND CHEERS FOR EVERY OUT
• Trumpet-and-drum cheering, and a rising "ooooh" from the crowd as the ball climbs
• Fireworks, cheers and applause every time you make the catch
• Air horns for diving catches and home run robberies
• Extra fireworks every 10 outs in a row, and a big finale for a perfect game

■ EASY TO PICK UP
• Move with the direction ring, and use the buttons to dash, jump and dive
• Catching is automatic — your fielder reaches out with the glove as the ball comes in
• Turn on the landing guide (NAV) and a ring on the grass shows where the ball will land

■ FEATURES
• Ranks from Sandlot Player to Legendary Center Fielder
• Share your score and rank with friends (shows whether NAV was on)
• Plays offline (ads and sharing need a connection)
• English and Japanese
