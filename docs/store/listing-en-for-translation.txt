[Full description] (max 4000 characters)
You are the center fielder in a baseball stadium at night. The batter hits 100 fly balls to center field. Can you catch all of them?

■ HOW TO PLAY
• Watch the ball after the batter hits it. Find out where it will land.
• Run to the place where the ball will land.
• Some balls fly over your head. Start running back early.
• Some balls drop in front of you. Dive forward to catch them.
• Some balls go over the fence. Jump at the fence to catch them and stop the home run.
• In later rounds, the wind moves the ball to the left or right.

■ 3D VIEW FROM BEHIND THE PLAYER
• You see the stadium from behind the center fielder.
• You can see the batter, the pitcher, the crowd and the stadium lights.
• The shadow of the ball on the grass shows where the ball is.
• A small map shows the direction of the ball.

■ CHEERS AND FIREWORKS
• The crowd plays trumpets and drums.
• The crowd gets louder when the ball goes high.
• Fireworks go up every time you catch the ball.
• You hear an air horn when you make a diving catch or a jumping catch.
• You get more fireworks after every 10 catches in a row.
• If you catch all 100 balls, you see a big fireworks show.

■ EASY CONTROLS
• Touch the direction ring to move.
• Use the buttons to run fast, jump and dive.
• The player catches the ball automatically.
• You can turn on the landing guide. A yellow ring on the grass shows where the ball will land.

■ FEATURES
• Get a rank based on how many balls you catch.
• Share your score and rank with your friends.
• Play without an internet connection. Ads and sharing need a connection.
• English and Japanese are available in the game.
